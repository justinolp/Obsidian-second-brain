# MOC — Research

Research is evidence gathering, not automatically canonical truth.

## Workflow
Question → Scope → Retrieval → Evidence → Synthesis → Uncertainty → Implication → Verification → Persistence

## Template
[[00 - Second Brain/11 — Research Template]]

## Rules
- Record sources and provenance.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Record uncertainty.
- Promote system-impacting findings only after verification and regression.
